The core innovation

One canopy, five attachment modes

Start with Mode E, the slide-under platform. It is the most universal mode. It works on any bed with a flat underside and needs no rim thickness, lip, or surface texture. It expands until its curved outer arc clears the bed's rear corners and locks, so the canopy footprint follows the bed's size automatically. The dog's own weight is the fastener. You can order it with the canopy pre-attached as one piece, so there is nothing to assemble.

For travel, crates, or beds that get dragged around, use Modes A–D. Two included Rim Strips stay on the bed. Their underside ports take any of four tool-free hardware types with a quarter-turn bayonet, so you choose whatever fits your bed and can mix modes on the same bed. The canopy then presses onto the Rim Strips' Velcro loop face.

★ Lead mode · most universal

ESlide-under platform

A flat wire or plastic sheet, oval or rectangular, slides under the bed. The canopy attaches to its back edge with Velcro on the rear-left and rear-right cuffs, or with a rear flap, then drapes forward. The dog's weight is the fastener.

  • Any bed with a flat underside. It needs no rim thickness, lip, or surface texture.
  • Self-sizing. The platform expands until its curved outer edges clear the rear corners, then locks. The canopy follows the bed size through the platform's outer arc.
  • Grip layer on the bed side: a rubberized coating or a yoga-mat or shelf-liner type fabric.
  • Frame options: telescoping wire arms, a scissor pantograph (recommended, because it keeps the arc centered), or a flexible plastic ring with a ratchet slider. The lock is light duty with no spring-back: a friction collar, a pin-in-slot, or a ratchet.
  • Pre-attached option: the canopy ships on the platform as one piece, so there is nothing to assemble.

Canopy construction

A three-panel arc that fits round and rectangular beds

Two side wings join a center dome through a row of hinge knuckles along each seam. The hinge moves from a tight 120° curl for round and donut beds to a nearly flat 175° span for rectangular beds and crate pads.

  • 8 knuckles per seam, 0.375 × 0.75 in, spaced 2.0 in apart
  • 0.125 in / 3.18 mm stainless pin with friction washers
  • 1 in center fold gap, so the unit ships flat in the FBA small standard tier
  • Sizes S / M / L fit beds up to 24×18, 32×24, and 42×32 in

Shape memory

Wire ribs hold any angle you set

Each panel has internal channels with bendable 2.0 mm annealed steel ribs inside them. Bend the canopy once and it keeps that angle without sagging onto your dog.

  • 12 ribs total: 4 per wing at 11 in, 4 in the dome at 13 in
  • 4.0 mm channel ID · wings spaced 4.0 in / 101.6 mm apart
  • Paw-safe ends: 6 mm silicone caps, bar-tacked channel ends, 0.5 in fabric overrun
  • Fabric stack: 300D recycled oxford shell with DWR, 100 g/m² hollow-fiber batting, an optional perforated reflective scrim, and a minky lining

Velcro interface

Peel it off, wash it, and press it back on

The canopy edge carries a 1.5 in / 38 mm hook strip. The Rim Strips on the bed carry the matching loop strip. Clamps, straps, flaps, and discs stay installed while the canopy goes in the wash.

  • Hook run is the wing width minus 1 in (17 in on Medium). The loop runs 1 in longer.
  • Rated for 5,000+ peel cycles
  • Fold the fabric guard flap over the hook, then wash cold on gentle in a laundry bag with no softener. Tumble dry low or line dry. Ribs and knuckles stay in.

Why the platform leads. It is the cheapest complete attachment system, at about $0.42 versus $0.49–0.97 for A–D once their Rim Strips are counted, and it fits the most beds. Its only penalty is size and weight (6–9 oz), which adds about $0.15 inbound freight. That puts its landed cost within a few cents of the flaps. The clamps are the most expensive mode because of the springs, jaws, and pads. For suction, quality TPU discs cost about $0.17 more per set than cheap PVC cups, but cheap cups harden and lose their seal within weeks.
Pre-attached, one-piece option. The canopy can ship already Velcroed to the platform's rear cuffs, folded down onto the collapsed platform. The buyer slides it under the bed, expands it, and locks it, with no assembly. The trade-off is a pack of about 15 × 12 × 1.25–1.5 in, which goes past the 0.75 in cliff into large standard (about +$1.06 FBA, plus about $0.10 for a card insert). That leaves a margin of about 50% at $29.99, or price it at $34.99. See spec 6.5–6.6.
Shipping advantage. No bed ships with the product. The canopy folds at the hinge gap and is vacuum-compressed into a 0.75 in flat mailer, so it qualifies for small standard. Foam cave beds usually ship as large standard or large bulky.
The 0.75 in cliff. If the packaging grows past 0.75 in, the unit moves to large standard (about $4.71 incl. surcharge), which costs about $1.06 per unit in margin. The Large SKU ships as large standard and is priced at $34.99 for that reason.
Storage. 0.0724 ft³ × $0.78 per ft³ per month is about $0.056 per month, or $0.17 over 3 months. Gross margin is before PPC (10–15%) and returns (3–5%), which leaves a 30–37% contribution margin at $29.99.

Mode decision guide

Which attachment mode fits your bed?

Start with the floor. If the bed has a flat underside and stays put indoors, slide the platform under it (Mode E). For travel or crates, check the rim. If it's rigid, clamp it. If it's soft, strap it. If it has a lip, tuck it. If it's slick plastic, use suction. Modes can be mixed per corner.

Differentiation

Every competitor sells a whole bed. ThermoCanopy upgrades the bed you already have.

BarkBox/BARK hideaway beds, PetFusion covered beds, and the crowded Amazon field of cave, igloo, and tent beds all bundle the cover with the bed. These are the review themes we heard, paraphrased.

Gap 1 · Overheating

"The closed hood traps too much heat, and my dog pants and leaves the bed."

The front stays open, the wing angle meters the warmth, and the $15 mesh swap gives shade with airflow in summer.

Gap 2 · Collapsing hoods

"The hood sags flat onto my dog after a few weeks."

Wire ribs and friction hinge knuckles give the canopy a mechanical structure that stays where you set it.

Gap 3 · Can't wash it

"The cover is sewn around the frame, so I can only spot-clean it."

The canopy peels off the Velcro in one motion and goes in the washer whole, while the hardware stays on the bed.

Gap 4 · Not universal

"My dog loves his bed, and I don't want to replace it to get a cover."

Five attachment modes, led by a slide-under platform that fits any flat-bottomed bed, plus a flexing arc and three sizes, fit round, bolster, orthopedic, and crate beds.

Privacy screen for anxious dogs. Mount one wing plus the dome toward the busiest side of the room, or clamp it to crate bars as a partial cover. The dog gets a den with a view of the exit, which is safer than a draped blanket and less confining than a full cave.
