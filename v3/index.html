OverCover

A universal, detachable canopy and cover system that mounts on its own slide-under platform base.

  • ★ Slide-under platform is the only attachment
  • Three-panel arc with hinge knuckles
  • Bendable 2.0 mm wire ribs
  • Industrial hook-and-loop hem
  • Inverse arc-height hoops
  • Optional mesh panel SKU

What you are building. You are building a low, flat platform base that slides under a load zone (any flat-bottomed item the customer sets on it). Three half-moon wings extend on ratchet rails, and a five-hoop canopy mounts on hinge hubs at the side-wing tips. Its three-panel insulated cover is closed on the left, right and back and open at the front. The cover's hook-tape bottom edge presses onto loop tape on the platform's upper rim. Nothing else grips, ties or sticks to anything.

System overview · what ships in the kit

Three subassemblies and one optional SKU

OverCover is a detachable canopy/cover attachment system for a platform base. The factory builds three subassemblies. They mate with no tools, so the cover can be removed for washing without disturbing the base. The baseline is the Medium kit shown in the 3D demo. Its load zone runs from 30 × 24 in to 38 × 28 in (762 × 610 to 965 × 711 mm). Small and Large kits scale the same parts.

SUBASSEMBLY A

Slide-under platform base

A center load plate with grip top and floor feet. It has three half-moon wings (left, right, back) on six telescoping ratchet rails, and hub sockets at the side-wing front tips. Loop tape runs along the wings' upper rims.

SUBASSEMBLY B

Canopy frame

Two hinge hubs, each with a spring lock and pinned rib sockets. There are five hoops: two fixed-length (front 88.0 in, roof 90.0 in) and two shock-corded rear hoops, with hoop 0 in the rim channel. A fixed-length ridge tape and a front-hoop handle complete the frame.

SUBASSEMBLY C

Insulated three-panel cover

Left wing panel, center dome panel and right wing panel, joined by two rows of hinge knuckles. Bendable wire ribs sit in sewn channels. A 1.5 in hook-tape bottom hem with a guard flap, plus hoop wrap tabs.

OPTIONAL SKU

Mesh panel

A separate, interchangeable three-panel cover in 3D spacer mesh. It uses the same knuckles, rib channels and hook hem, and fits the same frame and platform rim.

Attachment method · the only one

How the slide-under platform attachment works

The idea in one sentence: the load sitting on the plate is the fastener. A flat center plate slides under the load zone. Three half-moon wings extend on ratchet rails until their loop-tape rims clear the load zone. The canopy hubs snap into sockets at the side-wing tips, and the cover's hook hem presses onto the wing rims. No part of OverCover grips, ties, sticks or bonds to the customer's item. The platform is the whole attachment interface.

  1. Slide under. The folded platform (24 × 20 in) slides under the load zone, grip face up.
  2. Extend. Each wing runs out on two ratchet rails in ½ in clicks until its rim sits about 2 in past the load zone.
  3. Mount and raise. Hubs snap onto the side-wing tip sockets. Swing the front hoop up and over until both hubs click.
  4. Seat the hem. Press the cover's hook-tape bottom edge along the loop tape on the wing upper rims.

1 · Load path

The load in the load zone presses the center plate to the floor. The grip top and silicone feet resist sliding. Each wing is tied to the plate by two rails. Canopy loads run from the hubs, through the side-wing tips and the rails, into the loaded plate. The platform lies flat with an envelope under 0.25 in, so it has no tipping point. The canopy weighs about 1.5 lb and its center of mass sits inside the footprint.

2 · Rails and ratchet

Six nested flat rails (two per wing) run into channels in the center plate. A spring-loaded detent latch clicks over ratchet teeth every 0.5 in. Each wing slides out freely but cannot creep back in. A thumb release lever retracts it. Travel is 6.0 to 10.0 in per side, with a riveted end-stop at 10.0 in. Each wing sets independently, so an off-center load still gets even rim overhang.

3 · Hub sockets and lock

The side-wing front tips carry molded hub sockets. Each hinge hub snaps in and can stay installed between uses. Hoops sit at 0, 22.5, 45, 67.5 and 90° about the hub axis, which is 0.9 in above the floor. When the front hoop reaches 90°, a spring pin drops into a notch and locks it. Squeezing both hub buttons releases it to fold.

4 · Hook-and-loop interface

Loop tape (1.5 in / 38 mm) runs along the upper rim of all three wings. Hook tape (1.5 in) is sewn to the full bottom edge of the cover. Hoop 0 lies in the rim channel, and hook-and-loop wrap tabs hold the cover to the other hoops. Peeling the hem and tabs frees the cover for washing. The frame and platform stay assembled.

5 · Three sides closed, front open

The cover runs down to the rim on the left, right and back, so rising warm air pools under the canopy. The front hoop is the opening. There is no floor crossbar: only the 0.25 in wing tips cross the threshold. The hubs sit 2 to 4 in ahead of the load zone, so the front hoop never presses on the load.

6 · No secondary fasteners

Nothing in the kit touches, grips or bonds to the customer's item. The slide-under platform is the only attachment. Every interface is platform-to-canopy: hub sockets and hook-and-loop on the wing rims.

Rib geometry · Rev C (unchanged from Rev B physics)

Inverse arc height: extension sets span, fixed hoops set height

The hoops never change length. Platform extension sets the hub span. The cover's hem is seated on the wing rims and the hubs sit on the side-wing tips, so extending the wings pulls each hoop's legs apart. That leaves less hoop length to rise, and the arc gets lower and flatter. Condensing the platform gives the same hoop length to spare, so it rises into a taller, tighter arc. No motor, spring or extra linkage is involved. The measured sensitivity is dh/dc = −0.56 in per in of span, so one 0.5 in click on one side changes the arc by −0.28 in.

Hard floor

The rail end-stop at 10.0 in per side caps the span at 30.4 in, so the arc can never drop below 24.3 in. That is 0.3 in above the 24.0 in design minimum.

Controlled return path

Hoops 3 and 4 (roof 90.0 in, front 88.0 in) are fixed length: the former top-center sleeve is pinned with one rivet. Rib feet sit in pinned hub sockets that swing ±15° in the arch plane. A fixed-length ridge tape (1 in webbing) ties all apexes on the centerline. Rib channels are sewn to exact rib length + 0.5 in.

Rear hoops and pre-curve

Hoops 1 and 2 keep a shock-corded slip joint: hoop 1 runs 101.8–105.4 in and hoop 2 runs 95.1–96.1 in. The cord always pulls them back to the shortest length the span allows. All hoops are pre-curved to the Standard shape, so they always bow up and never invert.

Physics, for the engineering file. For a fixed rib length L and chord c (the hub span), the circular-arc reference gives sin(t)/t = c/L and h = (L/2t)(1 − cos t). The doorway hoop is taller than it is wide (rise/span 0.8–1.3), so it behaves like a half-ellipse whose width is forced by the wings: a² + b² ≈ constant. Any increase in c uses rib length that would otherwise go into h. The 3D demo above runs the same numeric solve live.

Factory build sequence

How to build it

Build the three subassemblies in parallel, then mate them at final assembly and test each kit on a fixture at the condensed, standard and extended settings. The step numbers match the 3D demo where they overlap.

  1. A · Platform base

    1. Mold the center plate (A1) and three wings (A4). Check that the plate envelope with the grip layer is ≤ 0.25 in.
    2. Apply the grip layer (A2) to the plate top. Fit eight floor feet (A3) to the underside.
    3. Insert two nested rails (A5) per wing into the plate channels. Fit the detent latches and release levers (A6). Set rivet end-stops (A9) at 10.0 in of travel.
    4. Press the hub socket inserts (A7) into the side-wing front tips.
    5. Apply loop tape (A8) along each wing's upper rim and heat-stake or rivet it every 3 in. The loop run must exceed the hook run by 1 in.
    6. Cycle each wing 0 → 10 in and back. Confirm 20 clean clicks and a hard stop at 10.0 in.

  2. B · Canopy frame

    1. Cut hoop rods to length: front 88.0 in and roof 90.0 in (±0.1 in). Pin the top-center sleeves on hoops 3 and 4 with one rivet each.
    2. Assemble the shock-corded slip joints on hoops 1 and 2 and confirm their travel ranges.
    3. Seat the hoop feet in the pinned sockets of both hubs (B1) at 0 / 22.5 / 45 / 67.5 / 90°.
    4. Pre-curve all hoops to the Standard shape, so their rest state bows up.
    5. Fit the front hoop handle (B8). Verify the hub spring-pin lock at 90° and the squeeze-to-release.

  3. C · Three-panel cover

    1. Cut the shell, batting, scrim and lining for the left wing, center dome and right wing. Quilt each stack on a 2.0 in diamond grid.
    2. Sew the rib channels to exact rib length + 0.5 in. Insert the capped wire ribs (C9, C10) and close each channel with a bar-tack.
    3. Sew the knuckle webbing tabs into the seam bindings, alternating panel ownership. Thread one stainless pin per row (C7) with wave washers (C8). Leave the 1.0 in fabric-only fold gap at the center of each row.
    4. Sew the hook tape (C11) along the full bottom hem with a 0.5 in setback at each hub end. Add the guard flap (C12) and the pull tabs (C14).
    5. Sew the hoop sleeves and wrap tabs (C13) and the shirred side gussets (C15). Bind all edges (C5).
    6. Bar-tack the ridge tape (B7) on the centerline at each hoop station.

  4. Final assembly, test and pack-out

    1. Mate the cover to the frame: hoop 0 into the rim channel, wrap tabs on hoops 1–4, ridge tape to the apexes.
    2. Snap the hubs into a platform on the test fixture. Raise and lock the canopy. Press the hook hem onto the rim loop.
    3. Measure the arc height at 6.0 / 8.0 / 10.0 in extension. It must read 28.8 / 26.6 / 24.3 in (±0.5 in). Reject anything below 24.0 in.
    4. Retract from extended to condensed. The hoops must rise evenly with no twist and no fabric bunching.
    5. Fold flat: hoops nested at the back, wings retracted, cover folded at the knuckle fold gaps. Pack the platform and canopy flat in one carton. A pre-mounted one-piece pack-out (hubs left in their sockets) is allowed.

Cover construction · hinge knuckles

Three-panel arc: the seams follow the platform setting

The two side wing panels join the center dome along two seams, each with a row of hinge knuckles. As the platform moves from condensed to extended, the hub span widens and the arc flattens. The seams open from about 120° to about 175°, so the cover lies smooth on the hoops at every setting.

  • 8 knuckles per seam, 0.375 × 0.75 in, 2.0 in on center, PA6-GF15
  • 0.125 in / 3.18 mm stainless pin with wave-spring washers for constant friction
  • A 1.0 in fabric-only fold gap at the middle of each row lets the cover fold flat for pack-out
  • Seams sit at 28% of the hoop run from each side, giving wing / dome / wing ≈ 28 / 44 / 28%

Cover construction · shape memory

Bendable wire ribs in sewn channels

Each panel has internal channels holding bendable 2.0 mm annealed, PVC-coated steel ribs. They run crosswise to the hoops and hold the panel's shape between hoop stations, so the cover never sags into the covered space.

  • 12 ribs: 4 per wing panel at 11.0 in, 4 in the dome at 13.0 in
  • Channel ID 4.0 mm · wing-panel rib spacing 4.0 in / 101.6 mm
  • Safe ends: 6 mm silicone caps, channels bar-tacked 0.5 in beyond each cap
  • Fabric stack, outside to inside: 300D rPET oxford with DWR, 100 g/m² hollow-fiber batting, optional perforated reflective scrim, minky lining

Cover-to-platform interface · industrial hook and loop

Loop tape on the base rim, hook tape on the cover hem

Loop tape (1.5 in / 38 mm) is fixed along the upper rim of the platform's three half-moon wings. Hook tape (1.5 in) is sewn to the full bottom edge of the canopy cover. Peel the cover off to wash it. The platform and frame stay assembled.

  • The loop run is 1 in longer than the hook run, so the hook always lands on loop at any wing setting
  • Industrial high-cycle grade, rated 5,000+ peel cycles
  • Fixing: PSA backing plus heat-stakes or plastic rivets every 3 in on the wing rim
  • Care label: fold the guard flap over the hook, wash cold and gentle in a laundry bag with no softener, tumble dry low or line dry. Ribs and knuckles stay in.

Optional accessory · separate mesh panel SKU

Mesh panel: an interchangeable breathable cover

The mesh panel is built and packed as its own SKU. It is a complete three-panel arc in breathable 3D spacer mesh, built on the same pattern, knuckles, rib channels and 1.5 in hook hem. It fits the same frame and presses onto the same loop-tape rim, so the cover can be swapped without touching the platform or hoops.

  • 3D spacer mesh outer over an open-knit liner, with an optional light UV-reflective colorway
  • 0.08 in / 2 mm panels with the same rib channels, so the mesh holds the arc
  • Swapping covers takes under a minute, with no hardware touched
  • Pattern pieces are shared with the insulated cover. Only the fabric stack changes (no batting, no scrim).
